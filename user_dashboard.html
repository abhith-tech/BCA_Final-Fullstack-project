Welcome to EmpowerAccess

EmpowerAccess is a premium AI-powered accessibility suite designed to bridge the gap between complex technology and effortless daily use. Our system combines local rule-based automation with advanced Gemini AI to provide a responsive, secure, and intuitive experience.

Voice Command Engine

Our hybrid intent engine processes your requests instantly. Use the wake word "Hey Empower" followed by your command. Below are the supported categories:

🌐 Information & Web

"What's the weather in Mumbai?"
"Show me tech headlines."
"Check share price of Reliance."
"Search for healthy recipes."
"Play lo-fi music on YouTube."

💻 System & Apps

"Open Google Chrome."
"Close Notepad."
"Increase volume by 20%."
"Take a screenshot."
"What's my CPU usage?"

📂 Files & Folders

"Open my Documents folder."
"Create a new folder named Projects."
"Open Downloads."
"Set a timer for 5 minutes."

✨ Utilities & Fun

"Tell me a joke."
"What time is it?"
"What is the date today?"
"Hey Empower, say hello!"

Emergency SOS Protocol

Your safety is integrated into the core of EmpowerAccess. If you encounter an emergency, say "Hey Empower, emergency SOS" or "Help me". The system will:

  • Instantly determine your current GPS/IP location.
  • Send an automated SMS alert via Twilio to your registered emergency contact.
  • Log the event in the secure database for administrator follow-up.

AI Assistant (Gemini Flash)

When a command falls outside the predefined rules, the system seamlessly transitions to our Gemini 2.5 Flash integration. You can ask complex questions, request code snippets, or ask for explanations on any topic.

"Explain quantum computing like I'm five."
"Write a Python script to sort a list."

Software Updates

Keep your desktop application up to date by checking the "Download" tab regularly. You can also ask "Hey Empower, check for updates" and the system will notify you if a newer version (e.g., v1.0.4) is available for download.
